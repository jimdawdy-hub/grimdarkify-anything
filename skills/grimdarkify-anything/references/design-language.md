# Excess is the design brief

This is an over-the-top Warhammer 40,000 parody engine. Create a theatrical reimagining of the product with visual jokes, transformed characters, exaggerated materials, and typography that announces the faction before anyone reads the selector.

## Four visible obligations

The first viewport must contain:

1. **Faction typography.** Imperium gets enormous blackletter and decorated script. The bundled fonts are real: UnifrakturCook, UnifrakturMaguntia, and Pirata One. Chaos can use Metal Mania; Orks can shout through heavy stencils; T’au uses aggressively futuristic Orbitron. Plain system fonts remain appropriate for readable body text and functional labels.
2. **A transformed character or major illustration.** Reinterpret the original mascot, product illustration, or brand character. Example: Freecase's Nomo becomes a ridiculous flying servo-skull law librarian with cybernetic lenses, a judicial helmet, manipulator arms, and a law book. Design original art with an image tool when available. The same skull is not the answer for every faction.
3. **An environment.** A fortress-courthouse, deranged machine shrine, flying alien palace, scrap-built war engine, or living chitin chamber. Make the interface inhabit the world through architectural framing, depth, lighting and materials.
4. **Excessive ornament with a joke.** Wax-stamped status badges, skulls guarding a search box, absurd brass reinforcements, red emergency lumens, thick armored borders, parchment writs, cables around a settings panel, and labels treating ordinary app operations as matters of galactic importance.

The design succeeds when someone can identify both the original product and its ludicrous new faction identity without reading an explanation.

## Identity and variation

Use fresh entropy for ordinary runs. Vary large decisions: composition, the mascot's interpretation or staging, architecture, ornament placement, lighting, visual jokes, type treatment, palette, and material wear. Keep the selected faction recognizable. An explicit seed may replay a favorite; default runs should produce new visible work.

| Family | Go all the way |

## Parody while preserving function

Visible copy may become ridiculous. Accessible labels must still convey the real action. A button labeled “Invoke Judgment” can retain `aria-label="Search case law"`. A mascot can change species, construction, voice, and role. A decorative stamp must not falsely imply an actual legal determination, security status, or official endorsement.

Keep forms, routes, data, authentication and real operations intact. Clearly identify fictional example records. Keep body text readable, keyboard focus obvious, mobile controls usable, and reduced motion respected. Avoid flashing effects. Texture and ornament can be intense while the central action remains understandable.

Every shared image contains the unofficial/noncommercial small print inside the image. The notice belongs to the artwork, not just a surrounding caption.
