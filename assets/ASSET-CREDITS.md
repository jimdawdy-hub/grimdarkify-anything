# Original fan assets and fonts

All fan artwork is unofficial, non-commercial, and unendorsed by Games Workshop. No official logos or copied official illustrations are supplied. Warhammer 40,000 IP belongs to Games Workshop Limited.

`servo-skull.png` is an original image generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-11-13 for this parody. The original is preserved in the generation archive; this file is the project copy. The small-print notice is embedded in the image itself.

Prompt: A lavish, outrageously over-the-top gothic science-fiction servo-skull law librarian on a transparent background; ivory skull, blackened judicial helmet with generic scales of justice, ornate brass, red and blue cybernetic lenses, small mechanical wings, cables, parchment, a red leather law book, and manipulator arms. A painterly videogame-concept treatment, full silhouette, no official logo, copied character, Aquila or faction insignia. A bottom plaque reads “UNOFFICIAL · NON-COMMERCIAL FAN ART” and “Not endorsed by Games Workshop”.

`tribunal.svg` and generated vector decorations are original code-authored illustrations.

- [UnifrakturMaguntia](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/unifrakturmaguntia)
